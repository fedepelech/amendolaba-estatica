## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, boutique pastry atmosphere rooted in patience, warmth, and meticulous craft. The visual tone evokes the sensory delight of stepping into a refined European-style patisserie: freshly baked crusts, velvet chocolate creams, fragrant eucalyptus flourishes, and natural parchment surfaces. 

The target audience seeks elevated indulgence, celebratory confections, and handcrafted baked goods. Interactions must evoke feelings of comfort, trust, culinary craftsmanship, and quiet luxury.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Tactile Warmth** and **Contemporary Editorial Minimalism**:
- Silky, warm canvas tones eliminate clinical digital glare, creating an inviting printed-menu feel.
- Organic cocoa brown type provides soft, high-contrast readability without the harshness of pure black.
- Subtle sage accents celebrate natural botanicals and ingredients, grounding the rich caramel notes.
- Generous breathing room, deliberate typography pairings, and gently softened geometries establish modern, sophisticated digital spaces.

## Colors

The color system draws directly from fine pastry craft, grounded by warm cocoa roasts, flour-dusted marble counters, and delicate botanical touches.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#4A3024` / Rich Cocoa):** Serves as the primary anchor for core typography, interactive high-priority buttons, key active states, and focused brand elements.
- **Secondary (`#6D8B74` / Eucalyptus Sage):** Used for fresh seasonal accents, dietary badges (e.g., vegan, gluten-free, organic), order status indicators, and balanced contrast highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#C69B6D` / Toasted Caramel Gold):** Infuses gentle warmth into secondary buttons, subtle divider lines, rating stars, and pricing highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F5ECE1` / Warm Sand Beige):** Grounding neutral providing a soft, comforting base. Paired with pure clotted cream (`#FAF5EE`) for card surfaces and elevated containers.

### Application Guidelines
- Never use pure digital black (`#000000`). Substitute with cocoa tones (`#4A3024` for headings, `#5C3D2E` for body text) to retain an organic, hospitable atmosphere.
- Borders and dividers must utilize attenuated cocoa hues with soft opacity (`rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.12)`) or delicate caramel hairline strokes to avoid visual heaviness.
- Sage green badges should be rendered against very soft pastel sage tints (`#EDF2EE`) with saturated cocoa or sage label text.

## Typography

The typography pairs the literary elegance of **EB Garamond** with the clear, modern humanist proportions of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Brings heritage, artisanal authenticity, and bespoke craftsmanship to article titles, hero narratives, product names, and section headers. The letterforms recall custom patisserie packaging and classic salon menus.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances editorial warmth with clean, effortless legibility for long-form ingredient descriptions, nutritional values, shopping bags, order flows, and small data badges.
- **Micro-Copy & Badges:** Rendered in slightly tracking-expanded small uppercase or semi-bold sentence case via Plus Jakarta Sans to guarantee crisp, rapid reading even on low-luminosity displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is founded on an airy, unhurried rhythm reminiscent of curated luxury lookbooks and cafe tasting menus.

### Grid & Page Rhythms
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Content stacks vertically to offer expansive photography cards and focused checkout touchpoints.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces side-by-side product pairs and dual-panel checkout summaries.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with max-width constrained to `1280px`, centered with `margin-desktop: 3rem` and `gutter-desktop: 2rem`. Product grids operate predominantly on 3-column (4 cards per row) or 4-column (3 cards per row) configurations.

### Spatial Pacing
- Component padding relies on the `space-*` scale to maintain comfortable breathing room:
  - Form controls and chips: `space-sm` vertical, `space-md` horizontal.
  - Bakery product cards: `space-md` internal cushion, stepping up to `space-lg` in featured modules.
  - Section dividers: `space-xl` vertical margins to separate distinct culinary collections.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through layered tactile surfaces and diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadows rather than rigid physical drops.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Base Canvas:** `#F5ECE1` (warm sand beige), establishing an all-encompassing warm backdrop.
2. **Surface Container:** `#FAF5EE` (clotted cream), creating crisp, luminous product display blocks that lift gently off the warm base.
3. **Elevated Floating Layers (Drawers, Modals, Sticky Nav):** `#FFFFFF` with slight warm undertones, framing actionable user flows.

### Ambient Warm Lighting
- **Resting Cards:** Low-contrast ambient shadows tinted with cocoa: `0 4px 16px rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.05)`.
- **Interactive Hover:** Elements rise subtly with `0 10px 24px rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.09)`.
- **Popovers, Cart Flyouts & Modals:** Deep, velvety ambient diffusion using `0 20px 48px rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.14)`.
- **Ghost Borders:** In place of harsh dark lines, use soft border outlines (`1px solid rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.08)`) to gracefully hold card shapes against comparable tones.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a softened geometric language (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the natural, organic contours of handcrafted sourdough loaves, tarts, and rolled brioche.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (8px), balancing tactile friendliness with structured modern alignment.
- **Product Cards & Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px) corner radius, delivering a pillowy, inviting container for bakery photography.
- **Hero Containers & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px) corner radius for prominent editorial framing.
- **Badges, Chips & Counter Tags:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to mirror delicate botanical seeds and decorative sugar dragees.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Cocoa background (`#4A3024`) with clotted cream text (`#FAF5EE`). Hover transitions to deep cocoa (`#3A241A`) with smooth lift. Height: 44px to 48px, radius `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with caramel border (`#C69B6D`) and cocoa text (`#4A3024`). Hover fills with light caramel glaze (`rgba(198, 155, 109, 0.12)`).
- **Botanical / Action Highlight:** Eucalyptus sage (`#6D8B74`) fill with cream text (`#FAF5EE`) for order confirmations and cart callouts.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Botanical Badges:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Fill: soft eucalyptus (`#EDF2EE`), text: dark sage (`#3E5944`).
- **Filter Chips:** Clotted cream surface (`#FAF5EE`) with delicate cocoa border (`rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.1)`). When active, fills with solid cocoa (`#4A3024`) and cream text.

### Product Cards
- Built on `#FAF5EE` containers with `rounded-lg` (16px) corners.
- Images feature a subtle inner border and soft radius.
- Includes category kicker in muted caramel, title in `headline-sm` EB Garamond, and price in bold cocoa body font.
- Add-to-cart action floats in the bottom-right corner as a rounded circular button (`#4A3024` with cream icon).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Background: `#FAF5EE` on `#F5ECE1` canvas, or `#FFFFFF` within drawer surfaces.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.16)`. Radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state: `1.5px solid #4A3024` with a warm glow ring (`rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text: muted warm cocoa tone (`rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.45)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Base: `1px solid rgba(74, 48, 36, 0.25)`.
- Checked state: solid cocoa (`#4A3024`) with crisp white or cream check/dot. Radio buttons feature soft concentric inset padding.

### Specialized Bakery Components
- **Flavour / Allergen Icon Tags:** Minimalist pill indicators highlighting signatures such as *Gluten-Free*, *Sourdough Starter*, *Pure Butter*, or *Seasonal Fruit*.
- **Pastry Quantity Stepper:** Pill-shaped cocoa-bordered counter (`- 1 +`) with tactile tap targets and warm creamy background.
- **Artisan Note Banner:** Warm parchment-tinted callout container bounded by a fine caramel border for pastry chef recommendations and storage tips.
